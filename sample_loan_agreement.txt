PERSONAL LOAN AGREEMENT

1. The Lender sanctions a loan of INR 1,00,000 to the Borrower for a period of 12 months at 10% flat interest per annum.
2. The Borrower shall repay the loan in 12 monthly instalments (EMI) of Rs 9,166 each, due on the 5th of every month.
3. A processing fee of Rs 3,500 and mandatory insurance premium of Rs 2,500 shall be deducted from the loan amount at the time of disbursal.
4. Late payment of any EMI shall attract a penalty of 3% per month on the overdue amount, plus a fixed charge of Rs 500.
5. Prepayment or foreclosure of the loan is permitted only after 6 months and shall be charged at 5% of the outstanding amount.
6. The Lender may revise the interest rate or charges at its sole discretion with notice on its website.
7. If the Borrower defaults on two EMIs, the entire outstanding amount shall become immediately due and payable.
